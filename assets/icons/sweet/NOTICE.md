# Bundled Sweet artwork

Original, unmodified SVG artwork by EliverLara and the upstream contributors:

- **Sweet Rainbow** folder: https://github.com/EliverLara/Sweet-folders
  commit `40a5d36e50437901c7eaa1119bb9ae8006e2fe5c`,
  `Sweet-Rainbow/Places/48/folder.svg`.
- **Candy Icons** files/devices: https://github.com/EliverLara/candy-icons
  commit `83512fbcadcb7e1015ebbe1729a1894946b021be`.
  Sources: `mimetypes/scalable/{text-x-generic,image-x-generic,audio-x-generic,
  video-x-generic,application-zip,application-octet-stream,application-pdf}.svg`
  and `devices/scalable/drive-harddisk.svg`.

The SVGs are supplied in their editable source form, renamed to Orbit's
category names. `partition.svg` is an unchanged copy of `drive-harddisk.svg`.
They retain their upstream GPL-3.0 licenses, supplied in LICENSE-Sweet and
LICENSE-Candy. This small bundled subset does not install or replace a system
theme. Orbit's custom icons and thumbnail overrides still take precedence.
